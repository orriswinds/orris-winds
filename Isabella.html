Isabella Ackland

Isabella grew up in Bromley, southeast London and started learning the cornet at her primary school on a scheme called ‘Band on the Run’. This offered pupils the opportunity to have free group instrumental tuition and to perform in a concert. Completely hooked by the end of the term, Isabella then transferred to playing the horn the following year. Bromley Youth Music Trust was central to her musical development whilst at school and she had the opportunity to perform a range of repertoire and to annually tour Europe. Isabella went on to study at the Royal Academy of Music where she received a First Class degree with honours. She then pursued a Master’s degree at Guildhall School of Music and Drama on the Orchestral Artistry course, where she studied with Jonathan Lipton, Angela Barnes and Sue Dent. She graduated in 2024 with Distinction.

A successful orchestral freelancer, Isabella is currently on trial for jobs with BBC Symphony Orchestra, BBC National Orchestra of Wales, Royal Liverpool Philharmonic Orchestra and English National Ballet Philharmonic. She regularly works with Philharmonia Orchestra, London Mozart Players, Britten Sinfonia and Bournemouth Symphony Orchestra. Isabella was a member of Sinfonia Smith Square (formerly Southbank Sinfonia) 2024/2025 and was horn fellow with Philharmonia Orchestra 2025/2026.

Chamber music is central to Isabella's career and as well as performing regularly with Orris Winds, she is also a member of esteemed flexi-ensemble dectet Meliora Collective.

Outside of music, Isabella loves swimming, running, hiking and watching standup comedy.
